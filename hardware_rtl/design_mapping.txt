Design name  <->  RTL directory / top-level module
================================================================================

Every design is normalized to 512 MAC/cycle so that area and power are directly
comparable (see README.md). The "Design" column below is exactly the Design
column of results_28nm_iso512.csv.

Supporting blocks (not rows of results_28nm_iso512.csv):

  quantizer     src/quantizer                          MiX_MX_Quantizer
  k_smoother    src/k_smoother                         K_SMOOTH_ARRAY

Each design has a matching synthesis directory syn/<Design>/ and (optionally) a
SAIF power directory power_saif/<Design>/.

Notes
-----
* INT8, MXINT4_g16, MXFP4_g16 and NVFP4 are the block-32-PE variants used in the
  paper: the scale block size stays 16 (so the bit-width is unchanged) while one
  PE consumes two 16-element sub-blocks. This makes every design iso-PE-size at
  32 elements/PE, which is what makes the area/power comparison fair.
* MiX45b_MXINT4 and MiX45b_MXINT5 use the fused normalize+accumulate backend,
  matching the other MX-family designs (see README.md, "Fairness").
* FP16 is a dense 1-MAC/PE baseline, so it uses a 16x32 mesh to reach 512
  MAC/cycle; its CSV row is the 32x32 synthesis result divided by 2.
